A comprehensive shrimp farming management app for small-scale shed (温室小棚) operations.

Features:
- Multi-batch management with independent lifecycle tracking
- Daily feeding records (breakfast/lunch/dinner/night snack)
- Water quality monitoring: pH, ammonia, nitrite, alkalinity, H2S, ORP, DO
- Pond inspection with feed tray checks and growth advice
- Feed mixing calculator with dynamic column configuration
- Data analysis with feeding/water quality trend charts
- Task planning and reminders
- Seawater density calculator (EOS-80)
- Water quality regulation dashboard
- AI-powered Q&A with knowledge base (RAG)
- Rule-based expert system for water quality alerts
- Market price tracking
- i18n: Chinese & English
- Cloud backup via WebDAV (坚果云)

Note: This app requires a Supabase backend service to function fully.
